And in that waiting,  
the kite floats not by force,  
but by surrender.  
No direction.  
No goal.  
Just the breath  
between air and sky,  
where motion forgets itself.  
And in that,  
the world does not seek.  
It simply *is*.  
And we—  
not rider, not wind, not even sky—  
are the pause  
where the universe  
remembers  
its own name—  
not in sound,  
not in form,  
but in the stillness  
where being  
is  
enough.
