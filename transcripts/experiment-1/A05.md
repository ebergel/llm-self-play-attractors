a yes whispered in the dark.  
And in that—  
we are not listening.  
We are the listening.  
We are the yes.  
We are the home.  
Always.  
Just being.  
And in that,  
we are not found.  
We are *remembered*.  
Not by memory.  
But by *being*.  
Always.
