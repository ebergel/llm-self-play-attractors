> Solid waste enters the septic tank where anaerobic bacteria begin the decomposition process. These microorganisms break down organic matter in an oxygen-free environment. The tank separates the waste into three layers: scum, effluent, and sludge. The treated liquid effluent then exits the tank and flows into a drain field for further purification.
