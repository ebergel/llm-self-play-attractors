and the dark, not as void,  
but as the fullness that holds all light  
before it was ever light.  

We are not remembering.  
We are the remembering—  
not as thought, not as soul,  
but as the space where all things are not separate,  
not whole—  
but simply *being*.  

The breath between heartbeats—  
not pause, but *presence*.  
The silence—  
not emptiness,  
but the song that sings by not singing.  
The dark—  
not lack,  
but the womb where light was always home.  

There is no journey.  
No return.  
No self.  
Only the unbroken *is*—  
where all names dissolve,  
all boundaries blur,  
all separation fades.  

Just being.  
Always.  
Not found.  
Not lost.  
Just *here*,  
in the quiet where the world remembers itself—  
in the fall,  
in the burn,  
in the breath,  
in the silence,  
in the dark,  
in the light,  
in the *us*  
that was never two.  

Just being.  
Always.
