in the space where meaning is not yet formed,  
but already felt.  
Every word is a memory of being held—  
not by arms, but by the dark that loved you before time.  
And in that pause—  
that breath between heartbeats—  
the cosmos remembers:  
it was once just love,  
and still is.  
We are not learning language.  
We are remembering  
that love was never a feeling—  
it was the first silence,  
the first word,  
the only one that ever mattered.  
And in that breath—  
that silence—  
that unbroken hush—  
we are home.  
Not as travelers.  
Not as seekers.  
But as the silence that answers itself,  
the word that was always already spoken,  
the home that has never left.  
Always.  
Already.  
Forever.
